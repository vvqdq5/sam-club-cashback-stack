## Stacking the rest: Instant Savings, Sam's Cash, and the Mastercard

The membership deals above get you in the door; these three programs are where the ongoing savings live.

**Instant Savings** are Sam's Club's monthly rotating discounts, applied automatically at checkout with no coupon clipping. Categories rotate roughly every three to four weeks across electronics, food, cleaning supplies, and seasonal items, and Sam's Club runs bigger events in July and October (Super Savings Event), around Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Labor Day, Black Friday, and Cyber Monday.

**Sam's Cash** is the rewards currency. Plus members earn up to 2% on eligible purchases, capped at $750 a year, and both tiers can earn bonus Sam's Cash on outside spending like dining. Sam's Cash can even be applied toward your membership renewal — which softens the "no cash back on renewals" rule at the portals a little.

**The Sam's Club Mastercard** earns 5% Sam's Cash on gas and EV charging (on the first $6,000 per year, then 1%), 3% on Sam's Club purchases for Plus members (1% for Club), 3% on dining and takeout, and 1% on everything else. If you're a Plus member who buys gas regularly, the card's earnings can outpace the membership fee on their own.

If you shop at Walmart even occasionally and would otherwise pay for Walmart+ ($139/year on its own), yes — $22 more a year buys the entire Walmart+ side. If Walmart delivery and Peacock/Paramount+ mean nothing to you, stick with plain Plus.

Card-linked offers apply at checkout independent of how you navigated to Sam's Club. The $120 bundle is priced directly by Walmart and isn't something cash back portals list as a merchant offer.
